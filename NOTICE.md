# Notice

## Reference assets (`old/` folders)

The models and textures under every `old/` folder come from the project's current release. They are included only so collaborators can see what each replacement must cover: the object, its variants, its rough scale and its role in the game.

- Most of them originate from the original Decimation mod by BoehMod and contributors.
- Some block props originate from Doomsday Decoration (metadata credits "Administrator, MCreator").

The project owner holds permission to reuse these assets in the project. That permission does not transfer to collaborators and does not allow the assets to be reused, traced, retextured or published as new work. The original authors keep their rights. These files are being retired and will not ship once their replacement is done.
